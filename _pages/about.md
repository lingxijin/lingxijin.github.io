Lingxi Jin (金玲僖) is a Ph.D. candidate in [Educational Technology](https://my.ewha.ac.kr/et/) at [Ewha Womans University](https://www.ewha.ac.kr/ewha/index.do), under the supervision of [Prof. Hyo-Jeong So](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=dI0biU8AAAAJ&hl=ko). She expects to receive her Ph.D. in **August 2027**, with her dissertation defense scheduled for **May 2027**. Her research examines how AI can support learning without replacing the cognitive processes that learning depends on. Working across **Artificial Intelligence in Education (AIED)**, **learning analytics**, **human–computer interaction (HCI)**, and **learning experience design**, she studies how learners engage with AI support, how scaffolding influences problem solving and metacognitive processes, and how interaction traces can reveal learning processes and outcomes.

Lingxi serves as a reviewer for journals including *Computers & Education Open* and *Behaviour & Information Technology*, and as a **program committee member** for major conferences including **LAK 2027** and **ICHEC 2026**. She also served as Local Chair of the Festival of Learning 2026(co-location of AIED, EDM, and L@S).

Outside academia, she enjoys camping, climbing, and scuba diving, and holds an **Advanced Open Water (AOW)** certification.
