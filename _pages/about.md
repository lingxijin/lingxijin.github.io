Lingxi Jin (金玲僖) is a Ph.D. candidate in [Educational Technology](https://my.ewha.ac.kr/et/) at [Ewha Womans University](https://www.ewha.ac.kr/ewha/index.do), under the supervision of [Prof. Hyo-Jeong So](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=dI0biU8AAAAJ&hl=ko). She is a member of the [Interaction Design for Learning Lab (IDL Lab)](https://www.idlearning.org/) and holds an M.A. in Educational Technology from Ewha Womans University. Her research lies at the intersection of **Artificial Intelligence in Education (AIED)**, **learning analytics**, **human–computer interaction (HCI)**, and **learning experience design**, with a particular focus on designing and evaluating AI-supported learning environments.

Lingxi serves as a reviewer for leading journals, including *Computers & Education Open* and *Behaviour & Information Technology*, and on the program committees of major conferences such as LAK 2027 and ICHEC 2026. She served as the Local Chair of the Festival of Learning 2026.

Outside academia, she enjoys camping, climbing, and scuba diving and holds an Advanced Open Water (AOW) certification.

**She is currently seeking postdoctoral research and tenure-track faculty opportunities** in Artificial Intelligence in Education, learning analytics, and related fields. For academic collaboration, research opportunities, or inquiries, she can be reached at [jinlingxi@ewha.ac.kr](mailto:jinlingxi@ewha.ac.kr).
