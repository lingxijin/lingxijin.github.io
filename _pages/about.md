Lingxi Jin (金玲僖) is a Ph.D. candidate in [Educational Technology](https://my.ewha.ac.kr/et/) at [Ewha Womans University](https://www.ewha.ac.kr/ewha/index.do), under the supervision of [Prof. Hyo-Jeong So](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=dI0biU8AAAAJ&hl=ko). Her research lies at the intersection of **Artificial Intelligence in Education (AIED)**, **learning analytics**, **human–computer interaction (HCI)**, and **learning experience design**, with a particular focus on the design and evaluation of AI-supported learning environments.

Lingxi serves as a reviewer for journals including *Computers & Education Open* and *Behaviour & Information Technology*, and as a **program committee member** for major conferences including **LAK 2027** and **ICHEC 2026**. She also served as Local Chair of the Festival of Learning 2026.

Outside academia, she enjoys camping, climbing, and scuba diving, and holds an **Advanced Open Water (AOW)** certification.
