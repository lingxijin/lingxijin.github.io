Lingxi Jin (金玲僖) is a Ph.D. candidate in [Educational Technology](https://my.ewha.ac.kr/et/) at [Ewha Womans University](https://www.ewha.ac.kr/ewha/index.do), under the supervision of [Prof. Hyo-Jeong So](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=dI0biU8AAAAJ&hl=ko). She is a member of the [Interaction Design for Learning Lab (IDL Lab)](https://www.idlearning.org/) and received her M.A. in Educational Technology from Ewha Womans University. Her research lies at the intersection of **Artificial Intelligence in Education (AIED)**, **learning analytics**, **human–computer interaction (HCI)**, and **learning experience design**, with a particular focus on designing and evaluating AI-supported learning environments. She has been involved in research projects on LLM-based pedagogical agents, learning-by-teaching, AI-supported physical computing, and [educational recommender systems](https://www.idlearning.org/projects). She has also contributed to [Inha University's Next-Generation Semiconductor Talent Development Project](https://icee.inha.ac.kr/icee/index.do). Lingxi also contributes as a reviewer for leading journals, including **Computers & Education Open** and **Behaviour & Information Technology**, and serves on the **program committees** of major conferences such as LAK 2027 and ICHEC 2026. She also served as the **Local Chair** of the Festival of Learning 2026.

Outside academia, she enjoys camping, climbing, and scuba diving. She holds an Advanced Open Water (AOW) certification.

**She is currently seeking postdoctoral research and tenure-track faculty opportunities in Artificial Intelligence in Education, learning analytics, and related fields. For academic collaboration, research opportunities, or inquiries, please contact her at [jinlingxi@ewha.ac.kr](mailto:jinlingxi@ewha.ac.kr).**
