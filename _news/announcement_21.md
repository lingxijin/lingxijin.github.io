Our team received the **Academic Encouragement Award (학술장려상)** at the **2026 KAIM–KSET Joint Fall Conference** for our study, *LLM-Supported Physical Computing Problem Solving*. We examined how learners interacted with an LLM-powered conversational agent while troubleshooting physical computing tasks and how these interactions varied by problem type and where the problem occurred.
